I like making sense of complex things.

I'm a third-year Computer Science student specializing in Generative AI at K L University, Hyderabad. My work spans full-stack applications, computer vision, and RF signal intelligence.

At the Indian Army's 509 Army Base Workshop, I'm contributing to a counter-UAV detection platform. In my own projects, I explore how technology can feel more human — from maternal care to music controlled with a gesture.

When I step away from the keyboard, I pick up a pencil or a camera. Art and photography give me another way to observe, experiment, and tell a story.
